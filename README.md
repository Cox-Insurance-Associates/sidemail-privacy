SideMail Browser Extension

SideMail is a browser extension (Chrome and Edge) used by Cox Insurance Associates, Inc. staff. It shows the staff member's Microsoft 365 email next to Applied Epic, our agency management system.

What SideMail uses
- The staff member's Microsoft 365 email, contacts and profile, read and sent through Microsoft Graph with their permission when they sign in. This includes email content, attachments, names and email addresses.
- Applied Epic pages the staff member has open: the client shown, the client's email addresses, and attachments the staff member chooses to email or file.
- A sign-in token from Microsoft, kept in the browser's memory only and cleared when the browser closes.
- A few settings saved in the browser: how to open email files, the position of the panel handle, and which senders' pictures to always show.

Where it goes
Only between the staff member's browser, Microsoft 365, and our Applied Epic site. SideMail has no server of its own, sends nothing to anyone else, and does not collect analytics or usage data. Nothing is sold or shared.

Choices
Staff can sign out from SideMail's menu at any time. Removing the extension deletes its saved settings. Email remains in Microsoft 365, where Cox Insurance's policies apply.
